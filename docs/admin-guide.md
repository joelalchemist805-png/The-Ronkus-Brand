# Shop owner guide

## Sign in

Open `/admin` and sign in with your existing administrator email and password. There is no sign-up page. For your safety, the dashboard signs you out after 30 minutes without activity.

## Add a product and photos

Open **Products**, choose **Add product**, and enter the product name, price, category, and other details. Save the product, then open it to add photos. Choose clear JPEG, PNG, or WebP photos that are no larger than 5 MB each.

## Set product options

Open a product and use its **Options** section to add choices such as size or colour. Use the custom-order section for measurements or other details a customer needs to provide. Save each section when you are finished.

## Feature products

Open **Featured** to choose which products appear in the featured area and arrange their order. Save your changes when the list looks right.

## Run a promotion

Create a coupon in **Coupons** first if customers should receive a discount. Then open **Promotions** to create a message or add a flyer, set when it should run, and choose the coupon if needed. Check that the promotion is active and that its dates are correct.

## Handle an order

Open **Orders** and select an order to see its items, payment, and customer details. After payment is confirmed, prepare the items, arrange delivery or pickup, and update the order as each step is completed. Do not mark an unpaid order as paid yourself; payment is confirmed automatically.

Order statuses mean:

- **Pending** — the order was placed but payment is not confirmed.
- **Paid** — payment was confirmed.
- **Preparing** — the order is being made or packed.
- **Delivering** — the order is on its way or being handed over.
- **Delivered** — the customer has received the order.
- **Canceled** — the order will not be completed.

## If you are signed out

Return to `/admin` and sign in again with your existing administrator details. If you were working on a change when you were signed out, check the page before repeating it.
